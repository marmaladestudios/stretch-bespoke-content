Ingredient

Alpha Arbutin

What it does

Alpha arbutin is a stable, gentle skin-brightener that interferes with tyrosinase — the enzyme that triggers pigment production. It's well-tolerated by most skin types and a sensible first move before escalating to hydroquinone or prescription tretinoin.

Who it helps

  • Dark spots and pigmentation

How to use it

2% serum, once or twice daily after cleansing. Always wear SPF in the morning.

Pairs well with

  • Niacinamide
  • Vitamin C (L-Ascorbic Acid)
  • Hyaluronic Acid

Related ingredients

  • Niacinamide

    Calms redness, strengthens the skin barrier, and reduces visible pore size. One of the most well-tolerated actives — pairs with almost anything.

  • Vitamin C (L-Ascorbic Acid)

    Brightens, fades dark spots, and provides antioxidant protection. Best used in the morning under SPF.

  • Tranexamic Acid

    Targets melanin overproduction. Effective on stubborn dark spots and post-inflammatory marks.
