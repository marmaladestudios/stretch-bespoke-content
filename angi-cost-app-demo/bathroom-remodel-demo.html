What drives the cost of a bathroom remodel?

Three factors move the needle most: the scope (a cosmetic refresh vs. moving plumbing), the finishes you choose, and your location. Labor alone typically accounts for 40–65% of the total.

Tip: Moving plumbing fixtures (relocating a toilet or shower) is one of the biggest hidden cost drivers. Keeping fixtures in their existing spots can save thousands.
